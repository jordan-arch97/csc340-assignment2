Genji Shimada

Backstory

Genji grew up as the younger son of the Shimada clan, a crime family in Japan, but unlike his older brother Hanzo he never wanted to take over the family business. After years of tension between the two of them, a fight broke out and Hanzo nearly killed him. Overwatch found Genji and rebuilt his body with cybernetics, and while he was angry about it at first, training with the omnic monk Zenyatta later helped him accept who he'd become. He's now calm and disciplined, more interested in inner peace than getting revenge on his brother.

Abilities

  • Shuriken - throws three throwing stars at once
  • Swift Strike - dashes forward through enemies, resets on a kill
  • Deflect - blocks and reflects projectiles for a few seconds
  • Cyber-Agility - lets him double jump and briefly climb walls
  • Dragonblade (Ultimate) - a glowing energy blade for a short time
